## What has been resolved

- The stage is a real painted canvas, light and dark, with a grain overlay. It is flat, inset, and the panel runs off the bottom edge.
- The hero sits under the transparent nav with no hard seam.
- The hero and section type are at Fish's scale: 72/470 and 44/500.
- The page has three distinct section modes: centered hero and policy sections, the pastel pace split, the centered notes block, and the full-bleed dark Pro band.
- The loop is one divided strip, not three generic cards. The write line is visible at rest and types on hover.
- The Pro panel is a real product surface with real voice providers, a hands-free control, and a support chat.
- The pricing lists are balanced, the Pro card has a clear surface in both themes, and the close is a cream block with one black button.
- The old editorial rail, uppercase micro-labels, and section rules are gone.
- Mobile no longer clips the advance pill or the batch label; pricing and closing heads center.
- Copy was checked against the ai-slop list: no V or P clusters, no invented metrics, no not-X-but-Y, no em dashes for rhythm.

## Remaining risk

- At 1440x900 the first screen shows the top of the stage and the sample phrase; the advance pill sits just below the fold. Fish crops its product card too, but this is the one composition detail still under debate.
- The stage painting is generated for this mockup. A real client would replace it with licensed or commissioned art.
